Lessons applied

The internship made data quality, traceability, operator needs and reliability part of how I evaluate technical work.

Process

Observe the work

Understand handoffs, machines, registers and user needs before proposing a system.

Data

Identify useful signals

Focus on the measurements, delays and exceptions that support decisions.

System

Design for adoption

Build clear interfaces that reduce manual effort without adding unnecessary steps.
